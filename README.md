# Maid-Services-Management
After taking over as a GM of Maid Care Home Services in Baltimore, MD, I decided to modernize business operations. 
This is a custom-built business management system used to schedule cleanings, manage clients and staff, 
track payments, and measure operational performance, replacing a spreadsheet-based workflow with a purpose-built web application.

**Features**

Client management: full CRUD for client profiles, service frequency, assigned days, and notes

Scheduling: appointment calendar with two-way Google Calendar sync

Payments: Stripe integration for invoices, payment status, and webhooks, including payments recorded outside Stripe (cash/check)

Operational analytics: actual cleaning duration per client and per-client averages from imported GPS vehicle data, plus churn tracking based on payment and visit history

Role-based access: separate permissions for owners and staff

Bilingual UI: English and Brazilian Portuguese
